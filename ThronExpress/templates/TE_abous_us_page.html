About ThronExpress

The Urban Bathroom Sharing Platform. Redefining Privacy, Hygiene, and Accessibility in the Modern City.

Who We Are

Identity

ThronExpress operates at the intersection of urban technology and the sharing economy, providing high-standard private sanitation solutions.

Urban Focus

Designed for dense metropolises where public infrastructure fails to meet the basic needs of a mobile population.

P2P Model

Connecting trusted Hosts who share their personal space with verified Users seeking a premium, clean experience.

The Urban Challenge

In big cities, finding clean and accessible public toilets is a real nightmare. Public facilities are often neglected, unavailable at night, or non-existent in high-traffic zones.

Our Solution: We created a digital interface to bridge this gap. A reliable web and mobile platform ensuring trust, verification, and strict hygiene standards through a rigorous vetting process for Hosts.

The "Flush for Good" Program

We believe sanitation is a human right. For every session booked on our platform, a percentage of the fee is donated to international charities installing toilets in developing nations.
